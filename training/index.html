CDS program resources

Three things, whenever you need them

For attendants Orientation videos Eight short narrated modules on how CDS works, clocking in and out, and the rules that protect you and the person you care for. About an hour in total, and it ends with a completion record you can print. Start watching → For consumers Consumer orientation The same format for the person receiving care: your rights, your responsibilities as the employer, and how to orient the caregiver you hire. Start watching → Reference Handbook and pay schedule The CDS handbook covers policies, expectations and program standards. The pay schedule lists every pay week and payday for the year. Ask us and we will send both. Request a copy →

If you are the employer

Orienting your attendant

Missouri requires that you, as the employer, orient your caregiver on eight specific topics, and requires us to prepare you to do it. It sounds formal; it is one honest conversation with a checklist, and we help.
