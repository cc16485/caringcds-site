Employer setup guide

Two free numbers make you an employer

In CDS, your caregiver legally works for you. That is what gives you the control, and it comes with one piece of real paperwork: a federal EIN and a Missouri tax ID. Both are free, the whole thing takes about fifteen minutes, and this page is every step. Prefer to do it together? Call us and we will walk through it with you on the phone.

Do it with us: 417-218-2888
  1. 1

    Get your federal EIN, free at IRS.gov

    Apply online at irs.gov (search “apply for an EIN online”). It is free, takes minutes, and the number is issued immediately. Apply as an individual acting as a household employer, using your own name and Social Security number.

    Keep the confirmation letter the IRS shows you at the end, called a CP 575. Save it as a PDF and print a copy. If you ever lose it, the IRS can issue a replacement called a 147C, but the easiest audit you will ever pass is the one where you kept the original.

  2. 2

    Get your Missouri tax ID

    Register with the Missouri Department of Revenue as an employer for withholding. You will need the federal EIN from step one, which is why the order matters. Keep the Department of Revenue notice that confirms your Missouri number, the same way you kept the IRS letter.

  3. 3

    Send us both letters

    The state requires proof of both numbers in your case file within 90 days of your CDS start date, and state auditors check. Send us the CP 575 and the Missouri notice and we handle the rest of the filing.

The one mistake that fails audits

A W-9 does not count as proof. Neither does any form you filled in and printed yourself. The state accepts only documents the IRS or Missouri Department of Revenue produced with your name and number already printed on them: the CP 575 or 147C letter, a 940 or 941 return, or the Missouri DOR notice. This distinction is the single most common way families fail this requirement, which is why we ask for the letters and not a W-9.

The numbers are yours, forever

Your EIN and Missouri tax ID belong to you, not to us. If you ever change CDS providers, they move with you, so you never do this setup twice.

After setup, the filings are ours

Once you appoint us as your agent (IRS Form 2678, part of your enrollment packet), we file the federal and Missouri employer returns and your caregiver's W-2 on your behalf. You get the numbers once; we do the quarterly paperwork forever.

One thing that comes later

Once your caregiver has earned $1,000 in a calendar quarter, Missouri requires an unemployment insurance registration with the Division of Employment Security. Do not register early, the state will not accept it before the threshold. We track this for every consumer and tell you the moment it is time.
