Service area

CDS in Webster County, Missouri

We serve Webster County. If you have Missouri Medicaid and you need help at home, you can choose your own caregiver, including a family member or friend, and Medicaid pays them $15.00 an hour.

What you get

The same program, locally run

  • ✓You choose the caregiver. A son, daughter, grandchild, friend or neighbor. Not a spouse or legal guardian.
  • ✓$15.00 an hour, paid every Thursday by direct deposit or pay card.
  • ✓No cost to you. CDS is free to all Medicaid participants.
  • ✓We do the paperwork. Both tax registrations, payroll, taxes, background checks and EVV setup.
  • ✓Someone answers the phone. Monthly check-in calls, and an annual visit in person.
  • ✓No caregiver yet? We keep a list of people looking for work in Webster County and make the introduction.
