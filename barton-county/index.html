Serving Barton County, Missouri

Get paid to care for a loved one in Barton County

Missouri's Consumer Directed Services program lets people on Medicaid choose their own caregiver, including most family members and friends, and Medicaid pays that caregiver $15.00 per hour. Caring Companions has supported consumers and caregivers across Barton County and Southwest Missouri since 2017, from our Springfield office.

Check my eligibility Call 417-218-2888

Who qualifies in Barton County

You need MO HealthNet (Missouri Medicaid), a need for help with daily living, and the ability to direct your own care. Our two-minute check tells you where you stand.

Family can be the paid caregiver

An adult child, relative, friend or neighbor can be paid through the program. Spouses and legal guardians cannot. The full rules, plainly.

No cost, ever

CDS is free to Medicaid participants. We handle enrollment, the employer paperwork, payroll and taxes. Here is the whole journey.

Everything is done by phone and mail

No trip to Springfield required

Enrollment, employer setup and ongoing support all happen by phone, and our team comes to you for the visits the program requires. Living in Barton County never means driving to an office.
