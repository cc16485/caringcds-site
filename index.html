Who we are

Your trusted CDS provider

Caring Companions Consumer Directed Services is proud to support Missourians who choose self-directed care.

Through the state's Consumer Directed Services program, we help seniors and individuals with disabilities hire and manage their own trusted caregivers, often family members or friends, while Medicaid covers the cost of care.

Whether you are just learning about CDS or ready to switch providers, we make the process simple, clear and supportive. Our vision is for every eligible Missourian to know their options and have the freedom to choose the care arrangement that fits their life.

We check your eligibility quickly, guide you through the paperwork, and support you every step of the way, so you can focus on living independently with dignity and peace of mind.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Call 417-218-2888 and a real person will answer.

What is the CDS program?+

The Consumer Directed Services program lets you get help at home with daily tasks while staying independent. Best of all, you choose your own caregiver, such as a family member or friend, who can be paid for their time.

Who qualifies for the CDS program?+
  • Are 18 or older
  • Have active Missouri Medicaid
  • Need help with daily activities like bathing, dressing, meals or mobility
  • Live in your own home, not in a nursing home
Can I choose a family member as my caregiver?+

Yes. You can choose a trusted family member or friend. Legal guardians and spouses cannot be paid caregivers through the program.

How much will my caregiver get paid?+

$15.00 per hour, paid weekly by direct deposit or pay card. The pay week runs Sunday to Saturday and payday is the following Thursday. The rate is set by what the Department of Health and Senior Services funds, so we will tell you if it ever changes.

What kind of help can I get?+
  • Bathing, dressing, grooming
  • Meal preparation and feeding
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Medication reminders
  • Errands and grocery shopping
What if I don't have anyone to be my caregiver?+

That is not a problem. We keep our own list of people looking for caregiving work across the counties we serve, and we introduce you to someone near you. It is not a directory you browse: we match on your county and the hours you need, then make the introduction, and you still choose who you hire. Ask us to find someone.

Who pays the caregiver?+

Medicaid funds the care, and we handle all the paperwork, payroll, taxes and background checks. You direct the care and we take care of the rest.

How do I apply?+

To receive care, start here. To be paid as a caregiver, start here. Or call us on 417-218-2888, or toll free 866-863-5151, Monday to Friday 9am to 4pm.
