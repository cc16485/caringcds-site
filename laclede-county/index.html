Service area

CDS in Laclede County, Missouri

We serve Laclede County. If you have Missouri Medicaid and you need help at home, you can choose your own caregiver, including a family member or friend, and we pay them $15 to $16 an hour, every Thursday.

What you get

The same program, locally run

  • ✓You choose the caregiver. A son, daughter, grandchild, friend or neighbor. Not a spouse or legal guardian.
  • ✓$15 to $16 an hour, paid every Thursday by direct deposit or pay card.
  • ✓Paid through Missouri Medicaid. Approved Consumer Directed Services are paid for through Missouri Medicaid. There is no charge to the consumer for services and hours authorized by the state.
  • ✓We do the paperwork. Both tax registrations, payroll, taxes, background checks and EVV setup.
  • ✓Someone answers the phone. Monthly check-in calls, and an annual visit in person.
  • ✓No caregiver yet? Our private directory, Find CDS Caregivers, helps you meet caregivers who want to work in Laclede County.
