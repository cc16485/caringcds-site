Good to know

Four things that keep pay on time

Location on

The app checks you are at your consumer’s home when you clock in and out. It does not follow you between shifts.

Authorized hours

A shift cannot go past your consumer’s authorized hours. If a shift will not save, call us and we will help.

Clock out every visit

Clock out before you leave. A shift left open overnight cannot be paid until it is fixed.

Missed one?

Text us right away. Your consumer signs the EVV correction form by Saturday at midnight.

If it says this

“We can’t verify your location”

  1. Step outside or near a window, away from things that block the signal.
  2. Wait a minute or two, then try again.
  3. In your phone’s settings, let the app use your precise location.
  4. Still stuck? Call or text us at 417-218-2888. We can check the address on file.

App screens come from WellSky Personal Care caregiver guides, with practice names. Your app may look a little different. If something does not match, call us.
